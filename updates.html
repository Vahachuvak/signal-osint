SIGNAL / 04 ─ CHANGELOG

Tracking the signal.
What changed.

A record of the improvements made to Signal OSINT, from workspace updates to new features.

01 / CURRENT UPDATE

Choose your language. Explore the dark.

A more focused interface and a new way to follow the project.

  • Switch between English and Russian from the top of the site. Your preference stays in your browser.
  • A darker, terminal-inspired visual style across the workspaces.
  • This update log keeps the project's changes in one place.
02 / PREVIOUS UPDATE

More sources. More context.

The people-search workspace received clearer controls and additional public sources.

  • Bluesky, Mastodon.social, and Wikidata were added to the source selection.
  • Source presets, status filtering, and improved graph prioritization make results easier to explore.
  • The Media page now links to the Signal Telegram channel and donation jar.
03 / FOUNDATION

The Signal workspace.

Public-source people searches and passive website investigations in one place.

  • Explore results using relationship graphs and manual search links.
  • Save and compare browser history, and export reports in supported formats.
  • Results are not identity verification or a full security audit. Verify sources independently.

New entries will appear here as the project is updated.

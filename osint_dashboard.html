    About

    Transparency about sources and the limits of analysis.

    ◈   What is analyzed

    System DNS, HTTP response, TLS certificate, requested page HTML, and three public files: robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and security.txt. At most four GET requests are made to the site; redirects and discovered links are not followed.

    ⌘   External sources

    crt.sh, RDAP, and Internet Archive are enabled separately. The domain is sent to selected services; RDAP may redirect the request to the appropriate registry. Results may be incomplete or outdated.

    ◇   What Signal does not do

    Signal does not exploit vulnerabilities, test SQL injection, guess passwords, submit forms, or crawl websites. A lack of findings does not mean a site is secure.

    ◷   Privacy

    History stays in this browser. Cookie and form field values are not collected, and URL parameters are removed from reports. The site may reflect parameters elsewhere; do not enter secrets.

    Passive intelligence. Investigate only websites you have permission to examine. Data can be incomplete; results are not a security audit.
